A little closer to home.

Add MoleChores to your iPhone Home Screen.

  1. Open this site in your iPhone browser.
  2. Open the sharing menu. Depending on your browser and iOS version, you may find it inside the More menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If shown, leave Open as Web App on, then tap Add.
  4. Open MoleChores from its new icon and sign in if asked.

To receive reminders, open the installed app and enable notifications above. iPhone notifications require iOS 16.4 or later. You can use your tasks without enabling them.
