Import from spreadsheet

Upload Household_Tasks.xlsx (or a CSV exported from here) after filling in owners and hours. Rows are matched by Code.

  • Columns read: Code, Owner, Hours / Month, Notes, and optionally Frequency and Active.
  • Owner can be Chris, Andrea, Shared, or Needs Volunteer.
  • Blank cells leave the current value alone. Unknown codes are reported, not created.
  • Selected days: set Frequency to selected and Selected Days to a comma-separated list such as Mon,Sat,Sun. All seven days are available.
  • Exports include Sequence Steps: an ordered JSON list of task codes and delay_minutes. Referenced tasks must exist and have Frequency set to sequence. Use [] to remove a future sequence; active runs keep their steps.
